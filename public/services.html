Home Shifting

Relocating to a new home is an exciting milestone, but the process of packing and moving can be overwhelming. Our comprehensive home shifting service is designed to take the stress out of your move. We manage everything from securely packing your household items to safely transporting them to your new residence.

Our trained professionals use high-quality packing materials such as bubble wrap, sturdy corrugated boxes, and specialized wrapping for fragile items like glassware and electronics. We ensure that every item is carefully handled, reducing the risk of damage during transit.

Upon arrival, our team will assist in unloading and placing the furniture in your designated rooms, allowing you to settle into your new home swiftly and comfortably.

Key Benefits

  • Expert packing of fragile items
  • Safe and secure transit
  • Trained and polite staff
  • Door-to-door service

Domestic Relocation

Moving to a different city or state requires a logistics partner you can trust. Our domestic relocation services cover long-distance moves across India with an emphasis on safety and tracking. We utilize a wide network of national routes and robust transport vehicles.

Because items are in transit for days, we use multi-layer packing techniques to safeguard against road vibrations and impacts. We provide regular updates on the status of your shipment so you are never left guessing where your belongings are.

We also offer shared load (part-load) options for smaller moves, providing an economical alternative to booking a full dedicated truck for long distances.

Key Benefits

  • Pan-India coverage
  • Heavy-duty protective packing
  • Part-load and full-load options
  • Transit status updates

FAQ - Domestic Relocation

Bike Transportation

We provide specialized two-wheeler transportation services for motorcycles and scooters. Because bikes are prone to scratches during handling, we ensure comprehensive multi-layer packing using foam, bubble wrap, and corrugated sheets before loading them.

Bikes are transported in closed trucks, safely strapped to the side rails to remain upright and stable throughout the journey. We handle all types of two-wheelers, from standard commuters to heavy cruisers and sports bikes.
