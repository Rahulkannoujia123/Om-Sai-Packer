Cancellation Policy

At [Business Name], we understand that plans can change. This Cancellation Policy explains how we handle booking cancellations.

1. Cancellation Before Scheduled Date

If you need to cancel your moving booking, we request that you notify us as early as possible. Timely notification allows us to adjust our schedule and serve other customers.

2. Notice Period & Cancellation Charges

Cancellation charges may apply based on the notice period provided before the scheduled pickup time. The closer the cancellation is to the moving date, the higher the applicable charges may be to cover our preparation costs. Specific details regarding deduction percentages will be provided during the booking confirmation process.

3. Rescheduling Options

Instead of canceling, you may choose to reschedule your move. We allow reasonable rescheduling subject to availability. Please inform us in advance if you wish to change your moving date to avoid late rescheduling penalties.

4. Refund Process

If your cancellation is eligible for a refund of the advance payment, the refund process will be initiated in accordance with our Refund Policy. Deductions for cancellation fees (if any) will be applied before the refund is processed.

5. Company's Right to Cancel

We reserve the right to cancel a booking in the event of unforeseen circumstances, natural disasters, safety concerns, or if the customer provides false information regarding the inventory or access limitations. In such cases where the cancellation is initiated by us (not due to customer fault), a full refund of the advance will be provided.

6. Contact for Cancellations

To cancel or reschedule a booking, please contact our customer support team immediately via phone or email as provided on our Contact Us page.
