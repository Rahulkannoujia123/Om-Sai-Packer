Refund Policy

This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which [Business Name] processes refunds for our moving services.

1. Refund Eligibility

Refunds are applicable in scenarios involving valid cancellations (as per our Cancellation Policy), overpayments, or specific damage claims that have been approved by our dispute resolution team.

2. Advance Payment Refunds

If you cancel your booking within the allowed notice period, a refund of your advance payment will be initiated, subject to applicable cancellation charges. If our team is unable to fulfill the service due to our own operational failure, a full refund of the advance will be provided.

3. Non-Refundable Charges

Certain fees such as convenience fees, processing charges, or costs incurred for specialized packing materials already prepared/dispatched for your move are non-refundable.

4. Damage-Related Refunds/Claims

Refunds or compensation for damaged goods are governed by the transit insurance terms. Any claims must be registered within 24 hours of delivery with photographic evidence. The resolution of such claims is subject to investigation and approval.

5. Refund Process and Timeline

Once a refund is approved, it will be processed and credited back to the original source of payment. Standard processing time is typically 5-10 business days, though your bank may take additional time to reflect the amount.

6. Mode of Refund

All refunds will be transferred via digital payment methods (Bank Transfer, UPI, or Original Card Payment). We do not issue cash refunds.

7. Contact for Refund Requests

For any refund queries, please submit a formal request via email or contact our billing department through the information on our Contact Us page.
